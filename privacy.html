Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how BSandsCards Intelligence ("the Application") handles information accessed through eBay APIs.

1. Information We Access

When you authorize the Application to connect to your eBay account, the Application may access information made available through the eBay APIs and permitted by the scopes you authorize. This may include information relating to your eBay listings, inventory, orders, fulfillment activity, sales and marketing activity, analytics, account information, and financial information.

2. How We Use Information

Information accessed through eBay is used solely to provide the functionality of BSandsCards Intelligence, including helping the authorized user analyze, organize, and manage their eBay selling activity and sports card inventory.

We do not sell eBay account information or use it for unrelated advertising purposes.

3. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information accessed through the Application from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. Access to eBay account information is limited to purposes necessary to provide the Application's functionality.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent information obtained through eBay APIs. Information may be processed by service providers used to operate the Application and its infrastructure, solely as necessary to provide and maintain the Application.

5. eBay Information

The Application uses eBay APIs and is subject to applicable eBay developer terms and policies. The Application does not request or access eBay information beyond the permissions authorized by the user and configured for the Application.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Application's functionality and maintain associated records. Users may discontinue the Application's access to their eBay account by revoking the Application's authorization through their eBay account settings.

If you request deletion of information associated with your use of the Application, we will take reasonable steps to delete applicable information, subject to legitimate business, legal, security, and operational requirements.

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any updated version will be published at this URL with a revised effective date.

8. Contact

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy or the handling of information by BSandsCards Intelligence, please contact the Application owner through the contact information associated with the Application's eBay developer account.
